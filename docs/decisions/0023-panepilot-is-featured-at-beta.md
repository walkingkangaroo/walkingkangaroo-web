# 0023. PanePilot is the featured project, shown at Beta

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-14
- **Issues:** KAN-893
- **Round:** none: Grant's answers of 2026-10-14 in [docs/design-plan.md](../design-plan.md), recorded in [docs/rounds/hub-leads-with-panepilot.md](../rounds/hub-leads-with-panepilot.md)

## Context

[0002](0002-projects-shown.md) featured Common Ground and showed all five projects at Prototype.
PanePilot is the first Walking Kangaroo product to go live, at app.panepilot.au in November 2026.
Grant chose to lead the hub with it.

## Decision

- PanePilot is the featured project (`featured: true`). Common Ground is no longer featured.
- PanePilot is shown at Beta, step 4 of 5. It moves to Launch when it launches.
- The four games stay at Prototype and are listed after it.
- The five projects shown are unchanged. The rest of 0002 stands.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep Common Ground featured, and add PanePilot as a second hero:** two leads compete, and
  PanePilot is the one that's going live.

## Consequences

- `docs/brief.md`'s project table changes: PanePilot first, featured, at Beta.
- The home page is redesigned around PanePilot: [0024](0024-home-hero-is-the-fogged-pane.md) and
  [0025](0025-home-sections-panepilot-first.md).
- Changing PanePilot's stage stays a one-line change to its content file.
